# Research learning

Improve the next research decision, not the volume of notes. For choosing or running a new investigation, use [research](../research/SKILL.md).

Read [records](../../reference/records.md) when accessing the study. Read [external memory](../../reference/external-memory.md) only when a provider is supplied or already authoritative. Retrieve relevant lessons, their original evidence, contradictions, and superseded versions; a summary alone is not proof.

## Curate

- Compare successful and failed attempts on comparable cases. Separate failures of the idea, implementation, measurement, and unmet conditions. If the evidence cannot distinguish them, keep the explanation unresolved.
- Write each lesson as **conditions → action → expected mechanism**, with evidence, exceptions, and a next check. Record counterexamples. Label simulation-derived or retrospective lessons accordingly.
- Preserve original predictions and observations. Correct by adding a linked replacement; do not rewrite history to match the outcome. Conflicting results may require narrower conditions rather than choosing the latest or most favorable result.
- Promote a lesson into a default only when evidence supports its intended scope. A single success, saved reflection, or profitable stock is insufficient evidence of transfer. Do not count repeated copies of one source as independent support.
- Keep useful negative results and boundary conditions. Park weak lessons with an explicit decision and reopening condition. Keep rejected history retrievable; avoid loading it all by default.

## Check whether learning worked

For agent skills, distinguish **retention, retrieval, application, and transfer**. Test old and revised procedures on fresh comparable cases. Where useful, remove the proposed lesson to test its contribution. Check affected neighboring tasks and do not tune repeatedly on the final holdout.

For product or historical research, connect the lesson to the next decision and a future observation that could overturn it. For stocks, track business predictions separately from realized returns. Do not substitute a simulation for prospective outcomes.

Save concise rationale and links, not hidden reasoning transcripts. Use the record utility for identity, link integrity, history, and export; it cannot certify that a lesson is true. A study lesson does not itself authorize changing a global skill, personal memory, or external system.

Report lessons added, narrowed, contradicted, or parked; cite the evidence; identify the next transfer check. If nothing reliable was learned, say so rather than manufacturing a rule.
